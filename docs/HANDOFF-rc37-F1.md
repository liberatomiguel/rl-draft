# rc.37 F1 handoff — market, valuation and renewals (`claude/rc37-market`)

1. **Catalog**: `listingFor` keeps the top 120 contracted (stars included) and tops the page up with signable players until 24 are signable, as the free agents do; prices, windows and rules unchanged. The dead `signableCapFreeAt` is removed (no cap changes: the line reaches 99 at rep 70).
2. **Valuation**: ONE helper, `economy.talentFactorsFor`, feeds `marketValueFor`, `computeSalaryAsk` and the readout. It prices the reach gap (the potential his arc still lets him close: `peakAge` + profile `plateauYears`, never the hidden peakOffset) in tiers: ×1.20 at gap 10 (= rc.36), ×1.40 at 15, ×1.70 at 20, ×2.34 at 28. A 70/98 is 1.67× a 70/85; the same talent costs the same at 17 and 21 (an u18 talent loses the 0.9 discount); an unreachable gap past the plateau is no longer paid. At 19+ with gap ≤ 10, prices are bit-identical to rc.36.
3. **For F2**: price any player with `marketValueFor(market.valueInputOf(p, careerSeed, seasonIndex))`. A 4-field call still compiles but gets rc.36's profile-free factors. A sale must honour `market.resaleLocked(p, seasonIndex, day)`: no sale in the signing window or the next one (Worlds window → next preseason). AI bids and the Blockbuster degrade already honour it.
4. **AI interest**: `bidWeightOf` adds the reach gap (8–30) at any age (70/98@17: 9 → 29). The daily chance keeps rc.36's formula but counts only players a bid may target (not pending, not locked).
5. **Renewals**: a player's first renewal at the club (`SquadPlayer.renewalCount` 0) and any renewal at tenure ≤ 2 stay rc.36. After that the floor goes 0.7/0.8/0.9 and the jump ×2.75/×3.0. Premiums are capped at ×1.45, and at ×2.2 ÷ repPremium over the rep-neutral price (worst single renewal ×4.35, rc.36 ×4.47). Title-window bug fixed: `CareerSave.userTitlesBySeason` counts titles per season with their day; a mid-season signing only counts titles won after he joined.
6. **DEV-04**: new careers stamp `bandModel` 3. The true potential sits at a uniform place in the band: the level-0 and level-1 places are uniform, and level 2 stays exactly 50/50 against earlier bands. The age cap applies only once the age clock binds. Careers from rc.36 or earlier keep their band.
